The Story 🌟

Ontime was born from a simple frustration — no single app did everything I needed. Smart alarms, real focus sessions, a calendar I could trust, and health tracking, all beautifully unified. So I built it myself, from the ground up, in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Every feature solves a real problem I personally faced. Ontime is my love letter to productivity.
